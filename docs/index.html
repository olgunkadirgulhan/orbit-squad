🪐 Orbit Squad

Orbit Squad publishes short animated space videos: the Sun, planets and moons are friendly characters who explain real space facts through light-hearted skits. All characters, scenes, music and sound effects are drawn and generated by our own code; facts come from public scientific sources such as NASA.

This site describes the publishing tool behind the Orbit Squad YouTube channel. The tool renders our own original animations and uploads them to our own channel using the YouTube Data API. It does not access any other user's data.
